# Changelog

## 2.0.1 — First stable public release

- Consolidates the tested SIN HQ 2.0 implementation for WALO 1.6.11 and UNISG Command & Operations v1.0.3.
- Adds the Red Forest mine headquarters, native services, furnished interior, crafting workbench and existing-mattress sleep interaction.
- Includes the medic's bypass route and local save/load sitting restoration, private bartender job, trade/marker integration, toolkit support and medical-kit task.
- Gates addon mechanics to Warfare ON / Story Mode OFF and preserves original storyline definitions.
- Includes the bounded DAO creation filter without deleting saved anomalies.
- Removes temporary medic diagnostic messages and excludes diagnostic components and development artifacts from distribution.

Gameplay was confirmed by the author on `2.0.1-test.2`. Stable 2.0.1 retains that behavior; release preparation removes diagnostic output only. It follows the existing 2.0.1 prerelease history rather than reusing a lower 2.0.0 version.
