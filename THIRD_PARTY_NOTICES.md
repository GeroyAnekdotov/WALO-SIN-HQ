# Third-party notices

SIN HQ integrates with Warfare ALife Overhaul 1.6.11, UNISG Command & Operations v1.0.3, S.T.A.L.K.E.R. Anomaly and GAMMA.

- The published 2.0.1 package contained a combined WALO/UNISG/SIN controller. SIN 2.0.2 ships no `warfare.script`; the required WALO installation supplies it, or the optional UNISG mod supplies its own override. No UNISG HQ implementation is bundled in SIN.
- The interior and seating integration adapt mechanisms from WALO and UNISG. Models, workshop behavior and other referenced assets are provided by the required installation rather than bundled as complete upstream distributions.
- Science Bunker Trade Inheritance and Dynamic Anomalies Overhaul remain separate companion systems. This package contains scoped integration code, not their distributions.

The project owner intends to provide these changes to the WALO author for integration. No project-wide LICENSE has been selected or added.
